Project

Avoider Game: High Noon

COMP 1020 Python pygame

A desert-themed game where a cowboy follows the mouse and dodges ten enemies that bounce off the walls. I added two power-ups: a health item that gives 1 to 3 extra lives, and a tumbleweed that slows all enemies to half speed for 3 seconds. The player has to choose which power-up to chase.

  • Uses pixel-perfect collision with masks.
  • Touching an enemy slowly lowers your life.
  • The screen shows your life and play time.
  • Power-ups appear about every 4 seconds (70% health item, 30% tumbleweed).
